Faith · Justice · Youth Leadership

Young voices. Catholic roots. A call to justice.

The San José Diocese Youth Social Justice Council brings together Catholic high school students to learn, listen, lead, and help shape the social justice ministry of our Diocese.

Join the Council Explore Our Work

Our mission

To form young Catholic leaders who understand social justice, listen to their communities, and help advance the common good.

  • Learn how Catholic Social Teaching applies locally and nationally.
  • Understand how lasting, systemic change takes place.
  • Partner with diocesan leaders to strengthen social justice ministry.
About the Council

A council built around youth voice and formation.

The Council is a diocesan youth leadership body made up of Catholic high school students connected to schools, parishes, and youth ministries across the Diocese of San José. Members gather for formation, dialogue, service opportunities, field experiences, and conversations about the issues affecting young people and their communities.

01 · LISTEN

Bring youth perspective

Make space for the questions, experiences, concerns, and ideas of young Catholics in diocesan social justice conversations.

02 · FORM

Learn the tradition

Build a grounded understanding of Catholic Social Teaching and the practical work of pursuing the common good.

03 · LEAD

Turn formation into action

Develop the skills to collaborate, communicate, organize, and contribute thoughtfully to the life of the Church and community.

What We Do

Formation first. Action with purpose.

Our work is designed to move from learning to reflection to responsible action. Members may contribute through several areas of Council life.

Leadership Formation

  • Public speaking and facilitation
  • Catholic Social Teaching formation
  • Retreats, reflection, and team building
  • Leadership development and succession

Educational Programming

  • Student workshops and teach-ins
  • Conversations on current social issues
  • Partnerships with schools, parishes, and community venues
  • Interactive learning and dialogue

Outreach & Partnerships

  • School and campus ministry relationships
  • Parish youth ministry outreach
  • Regional Catholic partnerships
  • Conference and retreat networking

Advocacy & Youth Voice

  • Youth perspective on diocesan social justice priorities
  • Legislative education and preparation
  • Community listening and issue research
  • Field experiences, including Sacramento advocacy work
Our Advocacy

Listen locally. Think broadly. Act faithfully.

The Council approaches social justice through a Catholic lens centered on human dignity, solidarity, the common good, and the responsibility to accompany communities facing hardship.

Issues addressed by the Council are informed by youth experience, community needs, Catholic teaching, and the Council's formation process.

Immigration Accompaniment, dignity, and community support
Mental Health Whole-person care and youth wellbeing
Housing & Poverty Human dignity and the needs of neighbors experiencing hardship
Creation Care Responsibility for our common home
Human Trafficking Protection, dignity, and awareness
Civic Engagement Learning how public decisions affect communities
ListenGather youth perspectives and learn from people directly affected by an issue.
LearnStudy Catholic Social Teaching, local context, and credible evidence.
DiscernReflect, pray, discuss, and identify responsible ways to respond.
ActPartner with the Church and community through education, service, and advocacy.
